# Changelog

## Unreleased

### New

- Ammo Pack now also refills the magazine when the player activates their Ultimate.
- Hitchhiker moves to the Augment slot and can be paired with any Enhancement. Press Crouch + Jump to attach to a nearby ally or detach.
- Reaching an ally or zombie with Grapnel Launcher sends out a wave that knocks back nearby zombies and deals 30 damage to each. Wave hits can trigger on-hit Enhancements.
- Telekinesis pushes zombies in a 20-meter cone around the crosshair. Each hit deals 35 damage and pushes forward with the same strength at any range. The push plays the Grapnel Launcher wave sound and has a 14-second cooldown, down from 15.
- Throwing a zombie with Wrestler now pushes other zombies within 10 meters and up to 90° off the crosshair, dealing 35 damage to each.

### Fixes

- Ammo Pack now works correctly for most heroes, except Wuyang, Mauga, Jetpack Cat, and D.Mon (Pilot Form).
- Downfallen (Sigma) gives players 1.25 seconds to interrupt its grab with crowd control or a hack. An interrupted grab releases the player without a throw or knockdown.
- Beacon of Healing now uses a yellow smoke aura on the player, matching the size of Super Nano and Dash, because the previous effect obscured players' view.
- If No Cooldowns is already active at ultimate start, Charge Battery refreshes it to 8 seconds when less time remains.
- The third-person camera temporarily gives way to the native view during emotes and the ultimates of Bastion, Junkrat, and Doomfist.

### Balance

- **Buff** — Grapnel Launcher's cooldown is now 12 seconds, down from 14.
- **Buff** — Bulking Season now loses 40% of its stacks on death, down from 50%. Remaining stacks are rounded down.
- **Buff** — Bulking Season now grants +0.75% max health per stack, up from +0.5%, and caps the bonus at +150%, up from +100%.
- **Buff** — Engineer gains 4% ultimate charge each time they give armor to an ally.
- **Buff** — Auspex Scan now increases damage taken by minibosses by 40%, up from 20%.
- **Buff** — Charge Battery now grants 8 seconds of No Cooldowns at ultimate start, up from 7.
- **Buff** — Overcharge now gains 1 energy per 2.7 HP healed, including health packs, down from 3 HP.
- **Buff** — Plague Doctor now grants +50% healing, up from +40%. The total bonus on infected targets is now +87.5%, up from +75%.
- **Nerf** — Born to Battle grants the first ability reset on a final blow. Earn the second with three more final blows before spending the first. Spending a reset clears any partial progress toward the second.
- **Nerf** — Jetpack Cat cannot use Frenetic Flight while holding a zombie with Wrestler.
- **Nerf** — Grapnel Launcher direct hits now deal 75 damage, down from 100.
- **Nerf** — Laser Barricade now knocks down large bosses for at most 1 second per contact.

### Optimizations

- Optimized Auspex Scan code.
- Reduced Workshop element count for Grapnel Launcher.
